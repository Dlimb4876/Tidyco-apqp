# Product Support

## What this page is for
Product Support defines how much support effort each product requires per production batch.
Those values are a core input to monthly capacity demand.

## How calculations use this data
- Monthly support demand = support hours per batch x number of planned batches in that month.
- If the production schedule changes, support demand changes with it.
- In Logistics, hours per batch is made from three inputs: Kitting + Booking In/Out + Product Movement.

## Effective dating and history
Support rates are effective-dated, so future changes can be planned without rewriting past assumptions.
Every applied change should include a reason so teams can audit why demand shifted.

## Good practice
1. Update support values with a clear effective date.
2. Enter a meaningful reason (for example: process change, cycle-time study, or scope increase).
3. Recheck next-month and quarter headroom in the capacity chart after applying changes.
