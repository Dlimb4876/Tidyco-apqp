# Logistics Capacity

## What this page is for
Logistics Capacity plans technician workload and support demand for logistics activity.
It uses the same core model as other streams, with logistics-specific wording and support inputs.

## Special logic for Logistics
Product support is split into three components:
- Kitting
- Booking In/Out
- Product Movement

Hours per batch is the sum of those three values.
That total is then multiplied by monthly batch counts from Production Schedule.

## Typical workflow
1. Keep Team and Holidays up to date.
2. Maintain product support component values with effective dates.
3. Review chart headroom and product-load outputs for the next months.
