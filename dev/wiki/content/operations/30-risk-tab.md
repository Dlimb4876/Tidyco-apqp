# Operations Risk Tab

## What this tab is for
The Risk tab consolidates the most severe risk signals across the entire system.
It elevates high-impact threats from individual project silos so leadership can focus on what needs intervention now.

## Before you start
- Ensure project managers and engineers are actively updating their Risk Registers and PFMEAs.
- Understand the organization's risk tolerance thresholds.

## Key components
- **High Severity PFMEA Items:** Process failure modes with a critical safety or operational impact (Severity 9 or 10).
- **High Score Project Risks:** Project-level threats with high impact and likelihood scores.
- **Stalled Mitigations:** High-risk items where the associated mitigation action is overdue or lacks an owner.

## The analytical process
1. **Prioritize by severity:** Always look at safety/critical (Severity 9-10) PFMEA items first.
2. **Review top project risks:** Check the highest-scoring risks from the NPI Risk Registers.
3. **Check mitigation status:** For every top risk, verify there is an active, owned mitigation plan.
4. **Escalate:** If a high risk has no action, or the action is stalled, follow up immediately with the project owner.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- **Looking only at numbers:** A medium-score risk with no mitigation plan is often more dangerous than a high-score risk that is actively managed.
- **Ignoring stale risks:** Risks that haven't been updated in months might have materialized into issues or disappeared.

## Quick example
| Source | Risk / Failure Mode | Score | Mitigation Status | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PFMEA (Project X) | Final torque tool failure (S=10) | RPN 200 | Open Action: Install interlock | J. Smith |
| Risk Register (Project Y) | Supplier Z tooling delay | High (16) | Overdue: Source backup supplier | A. Doe |
