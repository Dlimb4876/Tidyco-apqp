# Operations Actions Tab

## What this tab is for
The Actions tab provides a cross-project execution view, aggregating commitments from across the platform.
It ensures that critical tasks, especially overdue ones, do not get hidden or forgotten inside individual project portals.

## Before you start
- Understand that this view aggregates data; actions must be updated in their source location (e.g., Action Centre or NPI project).

## Key components
- **Overdue Actions:** Tasks that have missed their planned due date.
- **Unassigned Actions:** Tasks that have been logged but have no named owner.
- **Cross-Project Queue:** A unified list of open actions spanning PFMEA mitigations, risk responses, and general NPI tasks.

## The analytical process
1. **Attack the overdue:** Focus immediately on items that are past due. Determine if they are blocked or just poorly updated.
2. **Find the orphans:** Look for unassigned actions. An action without a specific owner will rarely get done.
3. **Review by owner:** Check if specific individuals are overloaded with critical actions across multiple projects.
4. **Follow up:** Use the source links to jump directly to the specific action and update notes or reassign as necessary.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- **Accepting vague updates:** Allowing actions to remain "In Progress" for months without substantive progress notes.
- **Using this to micromanage:** This tab is for identifying stalled critical execution, not for tracking every minor daily to-do.

## Quick example
| Action Description | Source | Owner | Due Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Complete supplier quality audit | Project Alpha (Gate 2) | J. Smith | 2026-03-01 | ⚠️ Overdue |
| Implement DC tool interlock | PFMEA (HVAC Base) | *Unassigned* | 2026-04-15 | ⚠️ Needs Owner |
