# Operations Flow Tab

## What this tab is for
Flow gives you a quick read of production movement: what is active, what has completed, and whether delivery is keeping to plan.

## What to look for
- Sudden growth in active work with no matching completion
- Repeated adherence drift over consecutive periods
- Areas where trend direction is worsening rather than stabilizing

## System links
Flow signals are based on production planning and execution status.
If this tab shows stress, open Production views to identify the exact batch or work-area bottleneck.
