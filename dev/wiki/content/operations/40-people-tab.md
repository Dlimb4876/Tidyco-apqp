# Operations People Tab

## What this tab is for
People shows where capacity pressure is building across operational and engineering streams.
It acts as an early warning radar for resource bottlenecks before overload impacts delivery dates or quality.

## Before you start
- Ensure all capacity streams (ME, PM, Logistics, Unit 2/3/6) have updated their team availability and holiday plans.
- Confirm the Production Schedule is accurate, as it drives product-support demand.

## Key components
- **Utilisation Gauges:** High-level percentage of available hours currently allocated to planned work.
- **Headroom Trends:** Forward-looking view of spare capacity (or deficit) over the next 1-3 months.
- **Stream Breakdown:** Individual panels for each major operational area (ME, PM, Logistics, Unit 2, Unit 3, Unit 6).

## The analytical process
1. **Scan the gauges:** Look for any stream where utilisation is consistently above the safe threshold (e.g., >85%).
2. **Check the headroom trend:** Is capacity pressure temporary (one bad month) or sustained?
3. **Identify the driver:** If a stream is overloaded, is it due to high task demand, heavy production batch support, or reduced availability (holidays/absence)?
4. **Intervene:** Work with stream managers to rebalance load, delay non-critical tasks, or secure additional resource.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- **Ignoring holidays:** A stream might look fine on paper, but a spike in annual leave can instantly destroy headroom.
- **Treating 100% as the goal:** Running any team at 100% utilisation leaves zero room for emergent issues or rework, guaranteeing delays.

## Quick example
| Stream | Current Utilisation | Next Month Headroom | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ME | 82% | +45 hrs | Healthy |
