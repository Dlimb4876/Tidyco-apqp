# Timing Plan

## What this page is for
Timing Plan shows whether project delivery is tracking to plan.
It helps teams coordinate phase activities, milestones, and dependencies.

## How to use it well
- Keep start/end dates realistic and regularly updated.
- Add milestone markers for gate readiness checks.
- Compare planned vs actual to identify slippage early.
