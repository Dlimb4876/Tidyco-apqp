# APQP Gates

## What this page is for
APQP Gates track readiness from Gate 0 to Gate 5.
They provide a structured check that evidence, controls, and approvals are in place before moving forward.

## What a good gate review includes
- Checklist completion status
- Evidence quality (not just box-ticking)
- Correct role-based sign-off
- Clear blockers if a gate cannot progress
