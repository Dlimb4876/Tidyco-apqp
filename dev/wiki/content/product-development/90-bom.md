# Bill of Materials

## What this page is for
BOM is the project resource list for what is needed to deliver and support production readiness.
It usually includes parts, tools, materials, consumables, and kits.

## Practical guidance
- Reuse existing parts where possible from the parts database.
- Keep classification consistent for reporting and planning.
- Link rows to process steps when traceability is needed.
