# PFMEA

## What this page is for
PFMEA is used to identify and reduce process failure risk before it becomes a production issue.

## Core scoring logic
- Severity (S): impact if failure occurs
- Occurrence (O): likelihood of the cause
- Detection (D): likelihood of detecting before escape
- RPN = S x O x D

Teams usually prioritize by high severity first, then high RPN.

## Practical workflow
1. Link each risk row to a process step.
2. Record the failure mode, effect, and cause clearly.
3. Add corrective actions with owner and due date.
4. Enter forecast O and D after action to estimate risk reduction.
