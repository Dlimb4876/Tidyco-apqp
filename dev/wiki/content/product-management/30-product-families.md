# Product Families

## What this page is for
Product Families group similar products so APQP setup can be reused more effectively.
They help standardize PFMEA starting points and reduce duplicate work.

## Good practice
- Keep family definitions meaningful and stable.
- Confirm products are assigned to the correct family.
- Review family-template fit when product scope changes.
