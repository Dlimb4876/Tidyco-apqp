# Product Families

## What this page is for
Product Families group similar products together so that APQP setup can be reused effectively.
They provide the structure for standardizing PFMEA templates and reducing duplicate engineering work.

## Before you start
- Look at your product catalogue and identify logical groupings (e.g., by function, risk profile, or manufacturing process).

## Key components
- **Family Name:** A broad category label (e.g., "Pneumatic Valves").
- **Assigned Products:** The specific products that belong to this family.
- **Template Library:** A collection of standard PFMEA failure modes, causes, and controls common to this family.

## The management process
1. **Define the Family:** Create a broad but meaningful category.
2. **Assign Products:** Ensure all relevant items in the Product Catalogue are linked to this family.
3. **Build Templates:** Go to the template library for the family and define the standard risks and controls.
4. **Apply to NPI:** When a new product in this family starts APQP, apply the family template to auto-populate the baseline PFMEA.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- **Too granular:** Creating a family for every minor product variation, which defeats the purpose of shared templates.
- **Too broad:** Grouping highly diverse products together, resulting in templates that are irrelevant to half the family.
- **Stale templates:** Failing to update the family template when a new risk is discovered in a specific project.

## Quick example
| Family Name | Assigned Products | PFMEA Templates |
|---|---|---|
| Air Conditioning | HVAC Base, HVAC Compact | Standard AC Risk Profile (24 items) |
| Pneumatics | Main Air Valve, Dump Valve | Valve Assembly Profile (15 items) |
