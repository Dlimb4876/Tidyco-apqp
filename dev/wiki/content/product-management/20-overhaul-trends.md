# Overhaul Trends

## What this page is for
Overhaul Trends visualizes how product overhaul activity and standard times change over its lifecycle.
It helps planning teams identify where demand is rising, stable, or falling, and tracks the impact of engineering changes.

## Before you start
- Ensure products have historical data logged (this often happens automatically when MCS approvals complete).
- Understand the context of recent engineering or process changes.

## Key components
- **Trend Chart:** A visual timeline showing the change in standard overhaul hours over time.
- **History Log:** A tabular record of every change, who made it, and why.
- **KPIs:** Summary metrics showing the total baseline shift.

## The analytical process
1. **Select a product:** Choose the specific item you want to analyze.
2. **Review the chart:** Look for sustained trend movement (e.g., creeping hour increases) rather than single-point spikes.
3. **Audit the history:** Check the reasons provided in the log for major shifts. Were they driven by scope creep, process improvements, or MCS actions?
4. **Adjust forecasts:** Use this historical reality to challenge or confirm future operational forecasts.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- **Ignoring the "Why":** Looking only at the numbers without reading the logged reasons for the changes.
- **Overreacting to single events:** Changing long-term capacity plans based on one temporary rework spike instead of a sustained baseline shift.
