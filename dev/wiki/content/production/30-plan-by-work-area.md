# Plan by Work Area

## What this page is for
Plan by Work Area shows load from an area perspective.
Use it to balance work between units and avoid local bottlenecks.

## What to look for
- Heavy due-date clustering in one area
- Uneven batch density across areas
- Areas where status progression is slower than expected
