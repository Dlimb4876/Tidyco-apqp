# Production Process Workflow

## Primary workflow
1. Create/update batches in [Schedule](../production/10-schedule.md).
2. Validate load by product in [Plan by Product](../production/20-plan-by-product.md).
3. Validate load by area in [Plan by Work Area](../production/30-plan-by-work-area.md).
4. Keep status progression current to maintain reliable operations reporting.

## Daily control loop
1. Confirm today's planned vs actual progress.
2. Check delayed batches and immediate causes.
3. Update statuses so downstream dashboards stay accurate.
4. Flag at-risk batches to Capacity and Operations.

## Weekly planning loop
- Review next 2-6 weeks for bottlenecks by work area.
- Confirm capacity assumptions still reflect real staffing.
- Pull forward or defer batches where overload is visible.

## Common process failures
- Batch status not updated after changes on the floor
- Over-reliance on one view (by product or by area only)
- Ignoring the knock-on effect on capacity planning
