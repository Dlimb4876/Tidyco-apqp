# Action Tracker

## What this page is for
Action Tracker is where commitments are assigned and followed through.
It is the operational bridge between identifying a problem and proving it has been resolved.

## What makes actions effective
- Clear owner
- Realistic due date
- Specific expected outcome
- Source context (gate, PFMEA, risk, or change)
