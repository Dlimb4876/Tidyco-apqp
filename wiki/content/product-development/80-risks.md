# Risk Register

## What this page is for
Risk Register tracks project-level uncertainty and makes mitigation visible.
It should answer three questions quickly: what could go wrong, how serious it is, and what is being done about it.

## Good risk-entry quality
- Specific risk statement (not vague labels)
- Consistent likelihood and impact scoring
- Named owner and mitigation plan
- Link to action items for execution
