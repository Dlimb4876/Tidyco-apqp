# Process Flow Diagram

## What this page is for
PFD documents the real process sequence, including decision branches and rework paths.
It is the structural backbone for PFMEA and Control Plan content.

## Good setup practice
- Keep step numbering clear and stable.
- Use explicit decision links so branch logic is unambiguous.
- Group large maps with section headers for readability.

## Why this matters downstream
PFMEA rows and controls are stronger when they are linked to a clear process map.
If a step changes in PFD, related PFMEA and Control Plan entries should be reviewed.
