# Product Catalogue

## What this page is for
Product Catalogue is the source list for products used throughout planning and APQP.
It should be treated as a controlled data set, not just a display list.

## Data quality essentials
- Keep product names and codes consistent.
- Maintain correct lifecycle status.
- Assign the right family so template behavior and reporting stay aligned.
