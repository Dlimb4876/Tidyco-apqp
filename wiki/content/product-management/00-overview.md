# Product Management

## What this area is for
Product Management is the master-data home for products, families, and parts.
If data here is clean, planning and APQP workflows across the platform are faster and more reliable.

## What is managed here
- Product catalogue
- Overhaul trend history
- Product families and template alignment
- Parts database

## Practical workflow
1. Create and maintain core product records.
2. Keep family mappings aligned to current standards.
3. Maintain reusable parts data for downstream planning and NPI use.
4. Review trend outputs and share signals with planning owners.

## Why data quality here matters
- Wrong product structure creates downstream planning errors.
- Missing family mapping weakens APQP template consistency.
- Poor parts data slows project setup and increases manual rework.

## How this links to other systems
- Capacity uses product data for support and load planning.
- Product Development uses products, families, and parts in APQP workflows.

## Simple quality checks
| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Every active product has a family | Keeps templates and reporting aligned |
| Product names and identifiers are unique | Prevents duplicate planning records |
| Parts records are complete and current | Reduces data clean-up in projects |
