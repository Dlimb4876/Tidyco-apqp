# Overhaul Trends

## What this page is for
Overhaul Trends shows how product overhaul activity changes over time.
It helps planning teams identify where demand is rising, stable, or falling.

## How to interpret it
- Look for sustained trend movement, not single-point spikes.
- Compare top products over the same date range when making planning decisions.
- Use results to challenge or confirm forecast assumptions.
