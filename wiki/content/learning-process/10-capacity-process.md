# Capacity Process Workflow

## Primary workflow
1. Open [Capacity Hub](../capacity/10-capacity-hub.md).
2. Choose the correct stream (ME, PM, Logistics, Unit 6, or Production views).
3. Update team availability and holidays first.
4. Update tasks and product-support assumptions.
5. Review utilization and headroom.
6. Record any required rebalancing actions.

## Monthly planning order
1. Confirm production batch outlook.
2. Validate product-support rates.
3. Validate task demand.
4. Review chart outcomes and pressure months.
