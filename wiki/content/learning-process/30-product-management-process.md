# Product Management Process Workflow

## Primary workflow
1. Maintain product master data in [Product Catalogue](../product-management/10-product-catalogue.md).
2. Keep family assignment current in [Product Families](../product-management/30-product-families.md).
3. Maintain reusable parts in [Parts Database](../product-management/40-parts-database.md).
4. Review trends for planning signals in [Overhaul Trends](../product-management/20-overhaul-trends.md).

## Data quality checkpoints
- Product code format is consistent
- Family assignment is present and correct
- Parts references are valid and reusable
- Obsolete records are clearly marked

## Monthly routine
1. Add or update new product records.
2. Validate family alignment for active projects.
3. Refresh parts mappings for current production needs.
4. Review trend signals and share any planning flags.

## Common errors
- Duplicating products with near-identical names
- Leaving missing family tags on active records
- Treating parts updates as optional
- Failing to communicate major master-data changes
