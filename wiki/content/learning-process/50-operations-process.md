# Operations Process Workflow

## Daily operational review flow
1. Start at [Operations Overview Tab](../operations/10-overview-tab.md).
2. Triage issues by severity and urgency.
3. Drill into the matching tab: Flow, Risk, People, Actions, or Forecast.
4. Route actions to owning teams and track follow-through.

## Weekly pattern
1. Compare trend direction, not just single-day values.
2. Confirm unresolved issues are linked to owned actions.
3. Escalate persistent red/amber conditions.
