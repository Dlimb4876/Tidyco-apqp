# Operations Flow Tab

## What this tab is for
Flow gives you a quick read of production movement: what is active, what has completed, and whether delivery is keeping to plan.
It helps operational leaders spot bottlenecks and schedule adherence issues before they impact customers.

## Before you start
- Confirm that shop floor teams are actively updating batch statuses in the Production Schedule.
- Know the expected delivery cadence for key products.

## Key components
- **Active Work:** Total number of batches currently "In Progress".
- **Completions:** Batches moved to "Complete" within the current period.
- **Schedule Adherence:** Comparison of actual completions against planned due dates.
- **Trend Charts:** Visual representation of flow over time.

## The analytical process
1. **Check active load:** Is the number of active batches growing faster than completions? (This indicates a bottleneck).
2. **Review adherence:** Are batches completing on or before their due dates?
3. **Identify problem areas:** If flow is worsening, look for specific products or work areas causing the drag.
4. **Investigate:** Open the Production views to find the specific stalled batches and work with the area manager to clear blockers.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- **Trusting stale data:** If operators aren't updating statuses to "Complete", Flow will look artificially congested.
- **Focusing only on volume:** Delivering 10 easy batches while 2 critical ones stall is not good flow. Look at adherence, not just count.

## Quick example
| Area | Active Batches | Completed (This Week) | Adherence | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unit 2 | 15 | 8 | 90% | Flow is healthy. |
| Unit 6 | 22 | 2 | 45% | ⚠️ Bottleneck building. Investigate specific batches. |
