# Operations

## What this area is for
Operations is the cross-functional control tower.
It brings together live signals from production, capacity, risks, and actions so teams can spot issues early and respond quickly.

## What each tab gives you
- Overview: high-level status and quick direction
- Flow: throughput and schedule health
- Risk: most serious risk signals
- People: capacity pressure across teams
- Actions: open and overdue execution items
- Forecast: forward-looking commitments and assumptions

## How this links to other systems
- Production feeds flow-related operational signals.
- Capacity feeds people utilization and headroom.
- Product Development and MCS feed risk and action context.
