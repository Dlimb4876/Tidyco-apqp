# Operations Forecast Tab

## What this tab is for
Forecast captures the forward view: expected demand, planned delivery, and key assumptions.
It is used in planning reviews to align operational commitments with likely workload.

## Good forecasting practice
- Keep assumptions explicit and dated.
- Update notes when commitments or demand expectations change.
- Link forecast changes back to the areas affected (for example production schedule or capacity).
