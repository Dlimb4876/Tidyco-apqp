# Operations Risk Tab

## What this tab is for
Risk consolidates the most important risk signals across the system.
It helps leaders focus on what needs intervention now, not just what is open.

## Where risk signals come from
- Product Development risk records
- PFMEA high-risk rows (for example high RPN)
- Linked action status where mitigation is late or stalled

## Escalation routine
1. Start with highest severity or highest RPN items.
2. Open the source context.
3. Confirm owner, due date, and next action.
4. Track follow-through in Action Centre.
