# Operations People Tab

## What this tab is for
People shows where capacity pressure is building across streams.
Use it as an early warning view before overload impacts dates, quality, or response time.

## What to watch
- Streams with sustained high utilization
- Rapid headroom drop from one month to the next
- Mismatch between workload growth and team availability

## System links
People is fed by Capacity planning outputs.
If risk appears here, open the relevant Capacity stream to adjust team setup, demand assumptions, or schedule timing.
