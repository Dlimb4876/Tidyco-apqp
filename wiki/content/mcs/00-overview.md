# MCS

## What this area is for
MCS (Manufacturing Change System) manages controlled change from proposal through approval and implementation.
It is used when a process, method, or supporting setup needs formal change governance.

## High-level lifecycle
1. Raise the change request.
2. Capture impact and implementation considerations.
3. Complete first-stage approval.
4. Execute implementation activity.
5. Complete final approval and close-out.
