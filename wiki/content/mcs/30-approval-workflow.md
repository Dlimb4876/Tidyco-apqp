# MCS Approval Workflow

## What this page is for
This page explains how a change moves through the MCS stage model with traceable approvals.

## Stage model
- Open
- Impact Assessment
- Approval 1
- Implement
- Approval 2

## What to capture at each stage
- Impact: scope, risks, dependencies, and expected time impact
- Approval stages: clear decision and accountable sign-off
- Implement: evidence of what was changed and when
