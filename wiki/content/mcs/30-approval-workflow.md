# MCS Approval Workflow

## What this page is for
This page explains how a change moves through the MCS stage model with traceable approvals.

## Stage model
- Open
- Impact Assessment
- Approval 1
- Implement
- Approval 2

## What to capture at each stage
- Impact: scope, risks, dependencies, and expected time impact
- Approval stages: clear decision and accountable sign-off
- Implement: evidence of what was changed and when

## High-level workflow
1. Create the request and define scope.
2. Complete impact assessment and proposed plan.
3. Route to first approval decision.
4. Execute implementation exactly to approved scope.
5. Route to final approval and close.

## Decision guidance
Approvers should check:
- Is the problem and root reason clearly defined?
- Are risks and dependencies documented?
- Is rollback or containment clear if implementation fails?
- Is evidence provided for what changed and what was verified?

## Calculations and checkpoints (detailed)
### Approval lead time
```text
Approval Lead Time = Approval Decision Date - Submission Date
```

### End-to-end cycle time
```text
MCS Cycle Time = Final Approval Date - Request Open Date
```

### On-time closure check
```text
On-Time = Final Approval Date <= Target Closure Date
```

Tracking these measures helps teams identify bottlenecks in assessment, approval, or implementation.
