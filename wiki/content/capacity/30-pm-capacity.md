# PM Capacity

## What this page is for
PM Capacity gives Project Management teams the same planning framework as ME, with PM-only data.
It is used to forecast coordinator and planning load before delivery pressure builds.

## What to review regularly
- Utilization trend for the next quarter
- Team baseline and daily hours
- Task demand from active projects
- Product-support demand linked to planned production batches

## Calculation notes
- PM and ME follow the same capacity model so results are comparable.
- Product-support demand is schedule-driven, so changes in batch plans can shift PM load.
- Headroom is the key early-warning number for planning risk.
