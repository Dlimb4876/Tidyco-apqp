# Capacity Hub

## What this page is for
The Capacity Hub is your entry point into the right planning stream.
Use it first so you are editing the correct team and demand data.

## Streams you can open
- ME
- PM
- Logistics
- Unit 6
- Production capacity views

## Typical use
1. Open Capacity from the main navigation.
2. Choose the stream card you want to review.
3. Check the page header to confirm you are in the correct stream.

## Why this matters
Each stream has its own team, task, and support data.
Opening the wrong stream can make it look like data is missing or incorrect when it is actually filtered by design.
