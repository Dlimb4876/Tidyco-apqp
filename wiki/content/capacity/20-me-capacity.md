# ME Capacity

## What this page is for
ME Capacity helps Manufacturing Engineering plan workload month by month.
It shows whether the team has enough hours for both project tasks and ongoing product support.

## What to check first
- Chart tab for utilization and headroom trend
- Team tab for baseline available hours
- Tasks tab for planned demand
- Product Support tab for per-batch support rates
- Holiday Planner for leave and non-working reductions

## How the numbers are built
- Available hours come from team setup and working-day assumptions, adjusted by holidays.
- Task demand is converted into monthly load from planned effort.
- Product support demand is calculated from support hours per batch multiplied by schedule batches in that month.
- Total allocated load is task load plus product support load.

## Practical routine
1. Review next 1-3 months in the chart.
2. If a month is overloaded, check whether the driver is tasks, support, or reduced availability.
3. Rebalance by rescheduling work, adjusting support assumptions, or changing team capacity.
