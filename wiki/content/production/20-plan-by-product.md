# Plan by Product

## What this page is for
Plan by Product groups scheduled batches under each product so you can spot product-level loading pressure quickly.

## How to use it
- Review products with many overlapping runs.
- Check whether date clusters are realistic for each product.
- Use it to prepare rescheduling decisions before congestion becomes urgent.
