# Production

## What this area is for
Production is where planned batches are created and reviewed.
It is the operational source for what will be made, when, and where.

## Why this area matters across the platform
- Capacity uses production batches to calculate monthly product-support demand.
- Operations uses production state to monitor flow and delivery health.
