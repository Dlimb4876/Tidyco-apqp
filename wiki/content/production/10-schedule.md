# Schedule

## What this page is for
Schedule is the main production planning table.
Each row defines a batch and becomes the source for downstream production, capacity, and operations views.

## Key data to keep accurate
- Product
- Work area
- Quantity
- Start and due dates
- Status

## Why this page affects other areas
Capacity Product Support demand is calculated from scheduled batch counts.
If schedule data is incomplete or late, capacity and operations outputs will be misleading.
